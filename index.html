About

An engineer's grounding in teaching, research, and academic service.

Dr. Khan has served the Department of Electrical Engineering at UET Peshawar's Bannu Campus since 2004 — first as Lecturer, then as Assistant Professor from 2009 — for 23+ years of combined teaching and research experience. His coursework spans Communication Systems, Digital Signal Processing, and Electronics & Circuits, alongside supervising undergraduate final‑year projects and postgraduate thesis research, and over five years of hands‑on work in the Embedded Systems Laboratory.
